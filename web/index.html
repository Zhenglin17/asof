Evidence-grounded · replayable · no look-ahead

A market agent that may only read what was public at the time, and must quote it.

Replayed evening by evening, it scans for post-earnings gaps, a language model reads the filing behind each one and answers take or pass, and code checks that every claim quotes, word for word, a passage that was public at that instant. Below: real replays, every cited judgement, and the guards against seeing the future.
